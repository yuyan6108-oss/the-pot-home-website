关于我们 About Us

一锅小筑位于 North York 的 Yonge Street，是一家主打家常中餐和暖锅类菜品的小店。我们以分量足、味道实在、适合朋友聚餐和日常吃饭为特色。招牌菜包括特色火锅鸡、砂锅羊蝎子、砂锅牛筋牛腩、特色小炒、手工水饺和馅饼等。支持堂食和外卖。

The Pot Home is a cozy Chinese comfort food restaurant on Yonge Street in North York. We are known for generous portions, rich flavors, and warm home-style dishes that are great for casual meals, takeout, and sharing with friends. Our signature dishes include Hot Pot Chicken, Lamb Spine Clay Pot, Beef Brisket & Tendon Pot, specialty stir-fry dishes, handmade dumplings, and Chinese pies. Dine-in and takeout available.
